## Background

The goal was to improve the mobility experience by creating a single platform that unites customer touch-points while differentiating the values of the different Volkswagen Group brands. The same platform, dressed for two very different audiences.

## Porsche

### Digital product concept

The Porsche side of the platform leaned into a lifestyle, getaway experience: on-demand features, services, and vehicles, framed around moments rather than ownership.

![Porsche homepage with a "Routine, left behind" hero image of a restaurant at dusk and a row of available vehicles below](/projects/digital-auto-services/das-porsche-home.png)
