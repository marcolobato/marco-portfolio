## Background

As an industrial designer with a strong background in human factors, it was very important for me to research how mobile devices would affect the ability for people to drive. For many years I explored with many partners and in academia for solutions to reduce driver distraction. After a couple years of designing and developing in-vehicle controls and interfaces, I decided to focus my work on integrating this device into the vehicle, finding technology to mirror the content of the phone on the display and creating applications and interactions that could be used as a reference to inspire developers. I also actively participated and helped inform the industry and government agencies to create driver-distraction guidelines.

## From research to product

### Research

After different collaborations with academia with groups like the Personal Robots Group and Senseable City Lab at MIT (see the AIDA project) and our own push to make hardware product out of it, it was clear that the biggest challenge was not the human-machine interface but the technology gap. People have trouble communicating with machines, and to make machines act like humans there's a lot of intelligence needed and cost involved. Fortunately today artificial intelligence is bridging the gap. Below, a journey of the different projects and products from which I have learned a lot.

## Products

Examples of ideas and research work that transitioned and made the leap from research into products.

### VW Beetle App

![iBeetle interior with connected mobile cradle](/projects/mobile-device-integration/mdi-vw-beetle-app.png)

Part of the iBeetle edition, the first connected cradle that powers the iPhone and connects to real vehicle data, shipped with a mobile application that can replace the dashboard gauges, help you stay on top of your social game, and help you listen to your Spotify music from within one app.

### Sound Journey App

![Family in a Volkswagen using the Sound Journey app](/projects/mobile-device-integration/mdi-sound-journey.png)

A car that makes music based on the environment and the way you drive. A MirrorLink application that showcases Volkswagen's high-end speakers on test drives at the dealer.
