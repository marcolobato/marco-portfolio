## Background

For CES 2017, Volkswagen wanted to show that the brand was opening up to the digital ecosystems customers already lived inside. We built an end-to-end customer journey around the Volkswagen Car-Net Alexa skill, letting attendees move from a living-room scene with Amazon Echo into a Volkswagen with Alexa onboard, all inside Volkswagen's booth.

## Components of the showcase

### Alexa skill

A Volkswagen Car-Net Alexa skill that lets customers interact with Volkswagen's connected services through an Amazon Echo. The skill is a conversation designed to help people plan their errands, surface helpful reminders along the way, and make sure their vehicle is ready to go.

### Booth

![Volkswagen booth at CES featuring an enclosed white living-room pod branded with Amazon Echo, with vehicle visualizations on screens in the background](/projects/vw-meets-alexa/vma-booth.png)
*The booth consisted of an enclosed living room so attendees could experience the Volkswagen Car-Net Alexa skill in a quiet, familiar setup.*

### Interactive screen

![Flat illustration of a person seated at a desk facing a monitor, with an Amazon Echo on the desk and framed pictures on the wall](/projects/vw-meets-alexa/vma-interactive-screen.png)
*An interactive screen played an overview of features and the vision for future services.*

### In-vehicle Alexa skill

![In-car infotainment screen with the Alexa wordmark, a map of Braunschweig, a "Your Latest Trick" media tile, and a phone panel](/projects/vw-meets-alexa/vma-in-vehicle.png)
*When Alexa is invoked in the vehicle, the microphone logo switches to the Alexa logo while she's speaking, and the surrounding ring animates as a waveform that shows which occupant is speaking.*
