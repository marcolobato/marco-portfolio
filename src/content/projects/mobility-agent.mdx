## Background

This is a snapshot of the research and product work that grew out of the team's goal at the top of this page: tools that get people around effortlessly so they can focus on the things they love. The centerpiece is OTTO, the One Touch Trip Orchestrator, a conversational mobility agent we prototyped for the connected vehicle.

## Design principles

To keep the team user-centered as the work scaled, I pulled memorable stills from the videos we filmed during the project and used them as visual reminders of our principles. Each still pairs a moment with the principle it stood for, grounding the team in the brand values and the audience we were designing for.

![Young driver smiling behind the wheel, with the overlay "Create an emotional connection between people and our products."](/projects/mobility-agent/ma-principle-emotional.png)
*Daughter's first drive. Using technology to connect parents with their children during their first years at the wheel.*

![Two friends meeting by a car in a driveway, with the overlay "Delight and surprise by intelligently anticipating people's needs."](/projects/mobility-agent/ma-principle-delight.png)
*Front seat is mine. Embracing playfulness, including playful rules like "shotgun".*

## OTTO, One Touch Trip Orchestrator

The conversational mobility agent for Volkswagen.

![Volkswagen interior with the OTTO wordmark and "One Touch Trip Orchestrator" overlaid on the dashboard](/projects/mobility-agent/ma-otto-cover.png)

### OTTO is car and trip smart

Control your vehicle, plan your route, and coordinate the people on the trip with you.

### A conversational interface improves usability and discoverability

Talking to the car surfaces features that would otherwise sit buried in menus, and gives drivers a faster way to reach them without taking their eyes off the road.

<video controls preload="metadata" playsinline style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px; margin-top: var(--space-md); margin-bottom: var(--space-xs); display: block;">
  <source src="/projects/mobility-agent/ma-otto-prototype.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
</video>
*Functional prototype using API.AI to power the natural language understanding, coupled with a virtual vehicle to demonstrate a conversation that switches between Bluetooth devices paired to the vehicle.*

### Multi-channel support for seamless communication

The user talks to OTTO the same way they talk to a friend, picking up the conversation on whichever device is closest at hand.

![Facebook Messenger conversation with the Volkswagen Group chatbot, showing a map of nearby ice-cream spots and follow-up messages about arrival time](/projects/mobility-agent/ma-otto-messenger.png)
*Facebook Messenger chatbot. A point-of-interest search initiated on Google Home continues on Messenger, where cards make rich information easier to review without navigating by voice.*

### On-boarding tutorial

A tutorial that helps people get familiar with a conversational interface before they ever step into the car.

<video controls preload="metadata" playsinline style="width: 100%; border-radius: 8px; margin-top: var(--space-md); margin-bottom: var(--space-xs); display: block;">
  <source src="/projects/mobility-agent/ma-otto-onboarding.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
</video>
*Mock-up of the agent's discoverability on Volkswagen's website. An interactive script with multiple-choice questions teaches users how to find and use our Google Home action and how to continue the conversation in the car, by visualizing the dialogue in real time.*
