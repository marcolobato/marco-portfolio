## Background

Proactively started and promoted a new internal initiative with a couple of colleagues to step back and re-think the way we tackled innovation. I lead a team in collaboration with Daylight Design in San Francisco, embedding myself in their office to learn from their design methodologies and get a fresh and external perspective of the future landscape for our company. Our perspective was informed by Volkswagen's trend research, technology research, and user research.

## Process

I learned different methodologies to process the data from the interviews to inspire user scenarios. We sketched different scenarios and generated storyboards to present at different feedback sessions with customers to collect feedback and validate our ideas.

![Project framework: Insights + Actions, Ideation Focus, Future Vision](/projects/future-of-mobility/fom-framework-icons.png)
*The project's three pillars: Insights + Actions (things we learned and how they guided us), Ideation Focus (identifying future opportunity areas for users and Volkswagen), and Future Vision (user experience moments and strategic impact).*

## Future Visions

![CO2 deposit and charge station next to a Volkswagen vehicle](/projects/future-of-mobility/fom-co2-station.png)

### Crowdsourced carbon capture vehicle fleet

Integration of carbon capture technology in our vehicles and a network of stations for depositing carbon while vehicles are charging.

![AR phone showing daily earnings from a vehicle sharing platform](/projects/future-of-mobility/fom-sharing-platform.png)

### The car that works for you

Create a sharing platform to capitalize on the growing sharing economy. Equipping the vehicle with features that enable new social experiences to help customers offset ownership and leasing costs. As a first step, popular services like package delivery, passenger pickup, parking rental, and other services could be implemented first.

![Volkswagen pop-up showroom with vehicle pods and outdoor seating](/projects/future-of-mobility/fom-popup-showroom.png)

### The right car at the right time

New subscription-based vehicle access models that account for monthly vehicle swaps to fulfill customers' occasional need for a different type of vehicle.
